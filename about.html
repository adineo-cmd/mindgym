About & method

What this is, where the material comes from, and why it works.

The method

The Mind Gym applies sports science's oldest formula — stress, recovery, progressive overload — to cognition. Every muscle group follows the same structure: understand the function, drill it at the right difficulty, recover, add load. The 12 laws are the operating principles; the weekly program is the template.

The sources

The mental model library is adapted and expanded from Mental Models Box. The training system — the 30 domains and their 2,279 techniques — is adapted from the OmniMind framework by adineo, which synthesizes cognitive science, sports psychology and behavioral research. Descriptions were rewritten for clarity and consistency; examples are ours.

Starter site vs. database

The gym, library and program pages are a deliberate curation — the highest-leverage slice a human can actually follow. The database keeps the full material without overwhelming the main site: every technique, every tier, browsable one sub-domain at a time. Start small; expand when a muscle asks for more.

Privacy & offline

There is no account, no server, and no analytics — nothing to track, because nothing is collected. The site installs as an app and works fully offline after your first visit.

How to use it well

Don't do everything. Pick two muscle groups that would change your life this quarter, run the weekly program as written, and read one mental model a day — forty-one days later you'll have the full library. Open a database domain only when its muscle is already trained and hungry.
