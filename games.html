Games & puzzles

Free games and tools, one set per muscle group, each tied to the brain region it trains. This is the fun volume of training — but it only counts as training with rules (below the list).

The daily 15

A ready routine: 2 min Human Benchmark (baseline) → 5 min Lichess puzzles → 5 min Zetamac arithmetic → 3 min Wordle or Connections. Total: fifteen focused minutes, every day.

Rules of deliberate play

Cap it at 15–20 minutes — more is entertainment, not training. Play at the edge of your ability (Goldilocks), where you fail sometimes. Record scores weekly; a flat score for two weeks means raise difficulty or switch games.

What the science says

Honest version: brain games reliably make you better at the game, and evidence for transfer to everyday cognition is modest. That's why this page is a supplement to the gym drills, exercise, and sleep — not a replacement. Stress plus recovery still equals growth.
