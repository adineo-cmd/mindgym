Start here

Two ideas run this whole system. Get them and everything else is just sets and reps.

A mental model is a way of thinking that works

It's a compact explanation of how some corner of the world behaves — why people copy each other, why projects eat all your time, why you can't stop a show at episode four. Learn one and you spot it everywhere. Learn forty-one and you carry a toolkit no situation can surprise.

The brain trains exactly like a muscle

Structured stress, then recovery, then slightly more stress. That is the whole formula. Focus, memory, processing speed, learning, emotional control, reading people, grit — each is a "muscle" with its own exercises, its own progression, and its own overtraining signs. You wouldn't train biceps with random flailing; don't train your attention that way either.

  1. Learn the technique. Pick mental models from the library — each tells you what it is, why it happens, and how to use it this week.
  2. Do the reps. Follow the gym exercises for each muscle group, on the schedule from the weekly program.
  3. Progress and recover. Add load with the rules in progression & recovery, guided by the 12 laws. When the starter set feels light, the full database is waiting.
