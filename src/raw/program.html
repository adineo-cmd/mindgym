    Overtraining the mind

    Brains plateau like bodies do. If you notice fog, irritability, dropping focus scores, or dread of practice — that's not weakness, that's volume creep.

    The fix: cut all drills by 50% for three days, protect sleep, keep only the fun ones. Come back fresh.

    Sleep is the other half of the workout

    Memories are consolidated during sleep, not during practice. Review for 10–15 minutes before bed, then protect 7–9 hours. Skipping sleep to train more is doing biceps curls with chopsticks.

    Never miss twice

    Missing one day is life. Missing two starts a new habit. If you fall off, the next session is not a punishment — it's just the next rep.
